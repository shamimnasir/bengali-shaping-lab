Bengali Shaping Lab

A tiny browser-based check for Bengali conjuncts, font fallback and line wrapping. It does not score fonts or replace real-device testing; it gives you the same text in four font stacks so you can spot differences.

Everything runs locally in this page. Your text is not uploaded.

Tip: resize the window or each card to inspect wrapping. Installed fonts vary by device and operating system.

How to use it

Try the same line on Android, Windows, macOS and a browser with a downloaded Bengali font. Check conjuncts, punctuation, numerals and narrow mobile widths. A browser cannot tell you whether a glyph is aesthetically correct; compare what you see with a fluent reader and the target device.
